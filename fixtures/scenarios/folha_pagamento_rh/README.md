# folha_pagamento_rh

A manufacturer's payroll: `cargos` (20), `funcionarios` (400), `dependentes` (534) and
`holerites` (1200, three months). Generated by `folha_pagamento_rh` in the people pack;
`preview.json` shows the first rows of each table.

What the data holds:

- **`cargos`**, a lookup of CBO codes, job titles and salary bands. The `keep_cargos` run asks to
  preserve it, the way a client would, and checks that the preserve reaches the
  `funcionarios.cargo_id` foreign key and nothing else.
- **CPF** masked or bare, always valid.
- **PIS/PASEP** masked (`120.54321.09-7`) or bare, with a wrong check digit in some rows, nulls,
  and 12 rows where the employee's CPF was typed into the PIS field. No generated PIS is also a
  valid CPF by coincidence, so a PIS cell that validates as a CPF is always one of those 12.
  Eleven bare digits look like a phone number to a naive detector.
- **RG** in the issuing state's layout: SP with its mod-11 check character (`12.345.678-X`),
  RJ dotted, MG prefixed (`MG-12.345.678`), bare digits elsewhere.
- **Bank** code, branch (`1234-5`) and account (`123456-7`) with mod-11 verifiers.
- **Salaries** inside each job's band; payslips whose INSS and IRRF follow the progressive
  brackets, with dependants deducted, so `liquido = salario_bruto - inss - irrf`.
- **Dependents** whose children carry the employee's last surname; most children born since
  2016 have no CPF.
- **Dates** of admission in ISO and Brazilian layouts; termination dates mostly null.

What the engine does today: PIS, RG, branch, account and the registration number are typed free
text and redacted to the same zero mask (`000.00000.00-0`), so every row keeps its layout but the
values collapse into a handful of distinct masks. Salaries are jittered with outliers in the
hundreds of billions. Low-cardinality categories (department, bank code, kinship, payroll month)
are redacted.

Not expressible with the current vocabulary, and so left as prose: PIS check-digit validity
(only CPF and CNPJ have a check-digit assertion); the payslip identity `liquido = bruto - inss -
irrf` (no cross-column assertion); and that a dependant's output surname no longer matches the
employee's (survival is judged within one table).
