# condominio_moradores

A residential condominium: `unidades` (192: four blocks, twelve floors, four flats a floor),
`moradores` (424), `veiculos` (93) and `visitas` (1200). Generated by `condominio_moradores` in
the people pack; `preview.json` shows the first rows.

What the data holds:

- **Units** with an internal intercom extension (`b0704`), area and parking spaces.
- **Residents**: owners, tenants and dependants. Minor dependants have no CPF, RG, phone or
  email. The number the intercom dials is a masked mobile, bare digits, or the unit's extension
  (`ramal b0704`).
- **Vehicles** in old and Mercosul plates, with their parking slot.
- **Gatehouse log**: each visit has the visitor's name, document (an SP RG or a CPF), optional
  plate and reason in their own columns, and a `registro` line the doorman typed in free form
  that repeats them, sometimes with the visitor's phone, upper-cased or not. A quarter of the
  visits are by forty regulars who recur under the same name and document.

What the engine does today: names, CPFs and the intercom phones are handled by type and nothing
survives; the log is redacted whole. The visitor document column is free text, the entry time
(`14/11/2024 18:32`) is not recognised as a date and is redacted to a single mask. Units, blocks,
extensions and parking slots are redacted, and the `ramal` rows of the intercom column lose
their layout. Regular visitors get a different pseudonym on each visit.

Left as prose: whether the free-text log should be scrubbed (identifiers replaced, the unit
references and wording kept) or redacted whole. Length-preserving redaction satisfies every
assertion the vocabulary has for it, so the choice cannot be pinned by an expectation yet.
