# clinica_prontuarios

A clinic's electronic records: `pacientes` (600), `medicos` (25) and `consultas` (1500).
Generated by `clinica_prontuarios` in the people pack; `preview.json` shows the first rows.

What the data holds:

- **CNS** (Cartão Nacional de Saúde), fifteen digits, spaced as on the card
  (`123 4567 8901 0000`) or bare: definitive numbers derived from an eleven-digit stem
  (starting 1 or 2) and provisional ones (starting 7, 8 or 9), with nulls and a few wrong last
  digits. Validity is the weighted sum of all fifteen digits modulo 11.
- **CPF** for four in five patients; the rest are identified by CNS alone.
- **`historico`**, a free-text history typed by reception and the doctor. It repeats the
  patient's first name, a contact phone or the CNS, and in about one row in six discloses
  sensitive data under LGPD art. 5 II: HIV status, pregnancy, a suicide attempt, alcohol or
  drug dependence, religion.
- **CID-10** codes on a Zipf-like mix: routine check-ups, respiratory infections, hypertension
  and diabetes dominate; a long tail includes pregnancy, HIV, breast cancer and schizophrenia.
- **Doctors** titled `Dr(a).` with a `CRM/UF 123456` registration.
- **Consultation time** as `2024-01-03 12:30`; prices with free consultations at zero.

What the engine does today: CNS, CID-10, doctor names and consultation times are typed free text
and redacted. CID-10 becomes `X00.0` everywhere (still code-shaped, but the diagnosis mix is
gone); consultation times become `0000-00-00 00:00`; the history is redacted whole, which hides
the sensitive findings. A free consultation's price of zero is jittered to a tiny negative
amount (`-9.6e-07`).

Not expressible today: CNS check-digit validity (the prefix check stands in); that a zero price
stays zero; that a pregnancy code stays on a female patient (no cross-table coherence assertion).
