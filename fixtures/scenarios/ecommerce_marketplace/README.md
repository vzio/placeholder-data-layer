# ecommerce_marketplace

A marketplace dump: `vendedores` (120), `compradores` (900), `pedidos` (2500), `envios` (one per
order that was paid) and `avaliacoes` (reviews of delivered orders), joined by integer keys.
Generated by `ecommerce_marketplace` in the commerce pack; `preview.json` shows the first rows of
each table.

What makes it a marketplace rather than a shop:

- **One document column, two documents.** `vendedores.documento` holds a company's CNPJ for
  `tipo_pessoa = PJ` and, for individuals and sole traders selling under their own name
  (`PF`, about a quarter), their CPF. Both come masked or as bare digits. An individual's
  `razao_social` follows the sole-trader convention of the name followed by the CPF's digits,
  and their `nome_fantasia` carries their first name (`Thaís Papelaria Criativa`).
- **Skewed sellers.** Sales concentrate on the first, largest sellers; the buyer side is
  uniform.
- **Shipments** carry the buyer's CEP (masked or digits), city and state, and a tracking code in
  the layout of its carrier: Correios `QB123456789BR`, Jadlog's fourteen digits, Loggi's
  `LGI-` code, Total Express's `TX…SP` reference. Cancelled and unpaid orders have no shipment.
- **Reviews**: a 1–5 star rating and an optional comment. Most comments are plain; some give away
  the buyer's full name, phone or email ("mandem a nota para …").

The scenario asks for every buyer and seller identifier to vanish, each seller document to come
back as the same kind of document in the same layout, joins to survive, and the marketplace's
business data (statuses, carriers, ratings, regional mix) to stay true.

What the vocabulary cannot yet say, kept here as prose:

- A review's comment may name its buyer, whose name lives in `compradores`, two joins away. A
  letter-fragment survival check across a foreign key would catch the name surviving; today only
  the digits of a phone (any row, `scope: column`) can be checked across tables.
- Company legal names (`PJ` rows) are redrawn as company names, while an individual's (`PF`
  rows) must go. An assertion restricted to the rows where another column has a given value
  (`tipo_pessoa = PJ`) would express that split.
