# varejo_clientes_pedidos

A retail e-commerce dump: `clientes` (820), `pedidos` (2000) and `itens_pedido` (about 4000),
joined by integer keys. Generated by `varejo_clientes_pedidos` in the seed pack; `preview.json`
shows the first rows of each table.

The mess a real customer table carries:

- **CPF** masked, bare digits, null, wrong check digits, one digit short, or a sentinel (`N/A`,
  `-`, `NAO TEM`, `nao informado`).
- **Email** built from the customer's name at weighted real provider domains, with upper-cased
  addresses, typo'd domains (`gmial.com`, `hotmail.con`), a missing `@`, nulls and sentinels.
- **Phone** masked, bare digits, E.164, null, sentinels, and `ramal` extensions; the area code
  agrees with the city, as does the CEP and the state.
- **Dates** of birth and of order in ISO and Brazilian layouts, with impossible dates
  (`2023-02-30`, `31/04/2022`, `00/00/0000`).
- **Names** with all-caps, all-lowercase, padded and mojibake (`JoÃ£o`) variants.
- **Duplicates**: twenty customers typed in a second time with the CPF in the other layout, the
  name in another case and the phone in another format.
- **Placeholders**: seven rows that are entirely placeholder (`Fulano de Tal`,
  `teste@teste.com`, `111.444.777-35`, `(00) 00000-0000`) and two real customers with
  `teste@teste.com` typed in.
- **Notes** (`observacoes`) that repeat the row's own CPF or phone.
- **Orders**: six reference a customer that does not exist; statuses and payment methods are
  skewed; totals are the sum of the items plus shipping.

The scenario asks for identifiers to vanish, formats, validity, nulls and joins to survive, and
business data (statuses, catalogue, totals) to stay true.
