# fornecedores_b2b

A B2B supplier registry: `estados` (the 27 federative units, keyed by the text abbreviation),
`fornecedores` (600, with `uf` a text foreign key onto `estados.sigla`) and `contatos` (about
1500 supplier contacts). Generated by `fornecedores_b2b` in the seed pack; `preview.json` shows
the first rows of each table.

What it carries:

- **CNPJ** masked, bare digits, an integer export that dropped its leading zeros (12 or 13
  digits in a text column), wrong check digits, null, and `ISENTO`.
- **Legal names** (`razao_social`) of invented companies, in title or upper case; one in ten is a
  sole trader (MEI), whose legal name is by convention the owner's name followed by the owner's
  CPF.
- **Commercial mailboxes** (`contato@`, `vendas@`, `financeiro@`) at the company's own domain,
  which are role addresses rather than placeholders; toll-free `0800` numbers.
- **Contacts** with masked or bare CPFs, personal or corporate emails, job titles, and three
  placeholder contacts.

Five runs share one seed: the default; `preserve_estados` (a lookup table preserved, its
foreign key propagated); `preserve_cnpj` (refused, the CNPJ being a direct identifier);
`force_preserve_cnpj` (the refusal overridden); and `profile`.
